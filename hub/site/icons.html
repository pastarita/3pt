Icons: thirty Phosphor glyphs for the app surfaces

The hand-drawn glyphs in nav.js are the identity set: one per leaf, and the design system keeps them (invariant I8). App surfaces need more than twenty icons for buttons, rows and states, so this is the utility set beside them: a thirty-icon subset of Phosphor (MIT), regular weight, vendored in phosphor.js with no dependency. Rules. Utility icons never appear on a hub card or in the sidebar; those stay hand-drawn. In Swift the same thirty map to SF Symbols by name (tag → tag, gauge → gauge.medium, git-branch → arrow.triangle.branch); the mapping table is the next step, not this leaf. Sizes: 16 in rows, 18 in buttons, 20 in headers, 24 in empty states. One colour per icon, taken from the text beside it.
